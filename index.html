ABOUT ME

Learning by building.
Solving practical problems.

My work connects programming, mathematics and the everyday need to keep useful records.

Shop Assistant grew out of a practical need in my spaza shop: capturing product information, understanding margins and knowing when stock was running low. I used Python and SQLite to bring those records into a desktop application with Excel exports.

My other projects explore financial mathematics, student-facing web interfaces and introductory machine learning. I also use guided projects to practise the workflow from Python and SQL to Power BI reporting.

I'm looking for graduate, internship or junior opportunities in data analysis, software development and business reporting, where I can apply these foundations and keep learning.
